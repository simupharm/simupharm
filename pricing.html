Frequently Asked Questions

Can I try all instruments before paying?

The free plan gives you full access to UV-Vis, Polarimeter, and PSA — enough to experience the quality of our simulations. Upgrade to Pro when you're ready for HPLC, FTIR, GC, and Mass Spec.

How does SimuPharma compare to Labster or PraxiLabs?

SimuPharma is 5× cheaper than Labster and 2× cheaper than PraxiLabs, while being far more pharma-specific. We use real physics engines (Beer-Lambert, Van Deemter, FFT) — not pre-recorded animations. Plus we have an AI lab agent no competitor offers.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes! You can cancel at any time from your billing dashboard. Your Pro access will continue until the end of your current billing period.

Does the institution plan include support?

All institution plans include dedicated support, onboarding training for faculty, and analytics reports suitable for NAAC/NBA accreditation.

Can I use SimuPharma on my phone?

Yes! SimuPharma is a PWA (Progressive Web App) — it works on any device with a browser, including smartphones. It even supports offline mode after first load.
