About SimuPharma

Redefining Pharmaceutical Education

This platform was created to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application — giving every pharmacy student hands-on experience with analytical instruments, anytime, anywhere.

Problem Statement

Advanced pharmaceutical instruments like HPLC, NMR, and Mass Spectrometers are incredibly expensive and require specialized maintenance. Many educational institutions have limited units, restricting hands-on access for students.

This leaves students with purely theoretical knowledge, unable to visualize the operational workflow of these critical tools before entering the workforce.

Our Objective

SimuPharma democratizes learning by providing a free, accessible, and interactive virtual platform. We aim to:

  • Provide clear, concise information on major pharmaceutical instruments
  • Offer visual aids like diagrams, animations, and videos
  • Create web-based simulators that mimic real operations
  • Reinforce learning through interactive quizzes
Our Purpose

Why We Built SimuPharma

Our Mission

Make pharmaceutical instrumentation education accessible to every student, everywhere — regardless of their institution's budget or equipment availability.

Our Vision

A world where no pharmacy graduate faces an instrument they haven't already mastered virtually — where confidence meets competence from day one.

Scope & Future Expansion

Currently, the platform focuses on foundational instruments like HPLC, FTIR, and Gas Chromatography. Our future plans include:

  • Adding more instruments such as NMR, Mass Spectrometry, and Dissolution Apparatus
  • Developing more complex simulations with variable parameters
  • Creating a "Virtual Experiment" module for sample data analysis
  • Translating content into multiple languages for global reach
