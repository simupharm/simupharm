### Standard Scoring Algorithm (Brooke 1996)
1. For odd-numbered items (Q1, Q3, Q5, Q7, Q9), score contribution = $	ext{User Response} - 1$ (range 0 to 4).
2. For even-numbered items (Q2, Q4, Q6, Q8, Q10), score contribution = $5 - 	ext{User Response}$ (range 0 to 4).
3. Sum the 10 score contributions and multiply by 2.5:
   $$	ext{SUS Score} = 2.5 	imes \left[ \sum_{i \in \{1,3,5,7,9\}} (R_i - 1) + \sum_{j \in \{2,4,6,8,10\}} (5 - R_j) ight]$$
4. Final score ranges from 0 to 100 points. Benchmark percentile curves follow Bangor et al. (2008) and Sauro & Lewis (2016).
