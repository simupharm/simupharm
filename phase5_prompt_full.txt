Perform a DEEP, OPEN-ENDED, EVIDENCE-FIRST scientific publication and innovation assessment of the ENTIRE SimuPharm platform.

9. Which directions require physical laboratory experiments.
10. Which directions can be validated using public experimental datasets.

This is a RESEARCH DISCOVERY, NOVELTY ASSESSMENT, SCIENTIFIC GAP ANALYSIS, AND PUBLICATION TRIAGE phase.

The final objective is to identify the smartest research direction BEFORE wasting time on unnecessary student recruitment, laboratory work, or implementation.
