2. EVIDENCE BASE & FORENSIC ENGINE AUDIT

In strict accordance with the non-negotiable code freeze, backend/science/hplc.py (144 lines) was audited mathematically and structurally without modification.

A. Mathematical Formulation of the Engine

  1. Van Deemter Plate Height:

    H = A + B/u + C*u = 2*dp + 0.05/u + 0.01*dp^2*u

    For standard dp = 5.0 μm (5e-4 cm), A = 0.001 cm, B = 0.05 cm·mL/min, and C = 2.5e-9 cm·min/mL.
    Theoretical Optimum Flow: u_opt = sqrt(B/C) = sqrt(0.05 / 2.5e-9) = 4,472.1 mL/min.
    Because u_opt is 4.47 Liters/min, the simulator operates entirely on the steep downward slope (B/u) across all analytical flow rates (0.2 to 2.5 mL/min). Higher flow rates reduce plate height, increase plate count, and increase resolution monotonically.
  2. Dead Time (t0):

    t0 = L / (10 * F)

    Empirical shortcut completely omitting column internal diameter (dc) and interstitial porosity.
  3. Retention Factor & Resolution:

    k_adj = k_ref * (100 - %B) / 40,   tR = t0 * (1 + k_adj)

    Rs = (tR2 - tR1) / w2 = [sqrt(L) / (4*sqrt(H))] * [ (k2 - k1)*((100-%B)/40) / (1 + k2*(100-%B)/40) ]

    Notice that dead time t0 cancels out completely from the resolution equation.

8. EMPIRICAL ANCHOR AUDIT: NIST SRM 870 & TANAKA DATABASE

Phase 5.1 proposed utilizing NIST SRM 870 and the Tanaka column characterization database as empirical validation anchors for Direction B. Forensic auditing of both benchmarks disproves their applicability.

A. Forensic Audit of NIST SRM 870

NIST Standard Reference Material 870 is an internationally certified test mixture (uracil, toluene, ethylbenzene, quinizarin, amitriptyline) designed to characterize column selectivity under a strictly frozen, single operating condition:

  • Mobile Phase: Fixed at 80:20 methanol/water containing 0.02 M potassium phosphate buffer, pH 7.00.
  • Flow Rate: Fixed at 1.00 mL/min. Temperature: Fixed at 23.0 °C. Column Dimensions: Fixed at 4.6 × 250 mm.
  • Variance Audit: Zero variance exists in operational parameters (F, %B, L, T).
  • Validation Verdict: A dataset with zero variance across operational parameters cannot validate multi-dimensional sensitivity derivatives, Sobol variance decompositions, or failure boundary surfaces. Claiming NIST SRM 870 validates GSA is scientifically indefensible.

B. Forensic Audit of the Tanaka Column Database

The Tanaka database compiles selectivity descriptors across >100 commercial C18 columns to quantify stationary phase chemistry (hydrophobic retention, methylene selectivity, shape selectivity, silanol activity).

  • Domain Mismatch: Tanaka descriptors characterize column manufacturing chemistry, whereas SimuPharm's engine models operational parameters (F, %B, L, dp). SimuPharm contains no parameters for ligand density or silanol activity.
  • Zero Operational Sweeps: Tanaka measurements are conducted under fixed flow rates and mobile phases. It provides no multi-factor grids of varying operational parameters.
  • Validation Verdict: The Tanaka database cannot validate SimuPharm's sensitivity surfaces. Direction B possesses zero independent empirical validation anchors. Kill Condition 7 is activated.
