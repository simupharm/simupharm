# PHASE 5.2 FALSIFICATION PROTOCOL & HYPOTHESIS REGISTER
## FORMAL SCIENTIFIC HYPOTHESES, INTERACTION THRESHOLDS & KILL CRITERIA

### 1. FORMAL HYPOTHESIS REGISTER

#### Candidate Hypothesis (H_candidate):
"Chromatographic separation performance exhibits nonlinear parameter interactions that are not adequately characterized by conventional one-factor-at-a-time (OFAT) sensitivity analysis, and global variance-based sensitivity analysis (Sobol decomposition) can identify interaction structures associated with separation robustness and failure regions."

#### Primary Null Hypothesis (H0_primary):
"Chromatographic separation performance in the tested simulation domain is predominantly additive, such that total parameter interactions account for less than 5% of total variance ($\sum (S_{Ti} - S_i) < 0.05$), and parameter rankings produced by Global Sensitivity Analysis (GSA) are identical to those generated by conventional OFAT or standard second-order Response Surface Methodology (RSM)."

#### Secondary Null Hypotheses:
- **H0_2 (Stability):** Sobol first-order ($S_i$) and total-order ($S_{Ti}$) rankings vary significantly across random sampling seeds ($CV > 20\%$) at computationally tractable sample sizes ($N \le 2048$).
- **H0_3 (Physical Plausibility):** Over 30% of the sampled global design space falls outside physical HPLC operating domains (e.g., hydraulic backpressure $\Delta P > 400$ bar or extreme retention times $t_R > 30$ min).
- **H0_4 (Information Gain):** Standard second-order polynomial Response Surface Methodology (RSM) explains $\ge 98\%$ of the variance in resolution ($R^2 \ge 0.98$), rendering global Monte Carlo variance decomposition scientifically redundant.

---

### 2. PRE-REGISTERED INTERACTION EFFECT THRESHOLDS
To prevent post-hoc rationalization of weak effects, interaction contributions ($\Delta S_i = S_{Ti} - S_i$) are classified strictly according to the following pre-registered standards:
- **Trivial Interaction:** $\Delta S_i < 0.02$ (Indicates purely additive linear/monotonic behavior; no GSA advantage over OFAT).
- **Weak Interaction:** $0.02 \le \Delta S_i < 0.05$ (Marginal cross-coupling; detectable only with large samples, negligible practical AQbD impact).
- **Moderate Interaction:** $0.05 \le \Delta S_i < 0.15$ (Meaningful cross-coupling; modifies local robustness contours, justifies factorial DoE).
- **Strong Interaction:** $\Delta S_i \ge 0.15$ (Major nonlinear coupling; severely distorts local sensitivity slopes, necessitates global exploration).
- **Total Model Interaction Metric:** $\sum_{i=1}^k (S_{Ti} - S_i)$
  - If Total Interaction $< 0.05$: Model is classified as essentially linear/additive.
  - If Total Interaction $\ge 0.10$: Model exhibits non-trivial multidimensional coupling.

### 3. THE 10 EXPLICIT KILL CRITERIA
1. **Kill Condition 1 (Trivial Interactions):** Total interaction sum $\sum(S_{Ti} - S_i) < 0.05$ or no individual parameter exceeds $\Delta S_i \ge 0.02$.
2. **Kill Condition 2 (Numerical Instability):** Parameter rankings fluctuate across independent quasi-random seeds or sample sizes up to $N=2048$ ($CV > 15\%$).
3. **Kill Condition 3 (Range Sensitivity Collapse):** Shifting from wide screening space to narrow operational space causes parameter sensitivity rankings to invert completely or interactions to vanish.
4. **Kill Condition 4 (Domain Implausibility):** The global design space is dominated ($>30\%$) by physically invalid operating conditions (e.g., extreme backpressure, precipitation, or unphysical retention).
5. **Kill Condition 5 (Redundancy Over DoE/RSM):** Standard second-order polynomial response surface captures $\ge 98\%$ of the variance with low prediction error, demonstrating that conventional DoE already provides all actionable information.
6. **Kill Condition 6 (Dominance of Model Artifacts):** Primary sensitivity drivers are traced directly to implementation simplifications (e.g., linear $(100 - \%B)/40$ modifier, $B/u$ Van Deemter dominance, stochastic tailing) rather than physical chromatography.
7. **Kill Condition 7 (Absence of Empirical Anchoring):** Neither NIST SRM 870 nor Tanaka datasets can provide multi-factor sensitivity validation, leaving the study without an empirical grounding strategy.
8. **Kill Condition 8 (Prior Literature Preemption):** Literature re-check reveals that Sobol variance decomposition of HPLC robustness has already been comprehensively solved on validated physical models.
9. **Kill Condition 9 (Lack of Actionable Translation):** Numerical sensitivity rankings cannot be translated into concrete, practical method-development decisions under ICH Q14.
10. **Kill Condition 10 (Mere Implementation Paper):** The study reduces entirely to "we ran a Sobol Python package on a pre-existing HPLC simulator," which top-tier analytical chemistry journals reject.
