About SimuPharma

Redefining How Pharmacy Students Learn

SimuPharma was born from a simple observation: pharmacy students rarely get hands-on experience with expensive analytical instruments before entering the workforce.

Our platform provides scientifically accurate, interactive simulations that let students operate virtual versions of instruments like HPLC, FTIR, UV-Vis and more — building confidence and competence before they ever touch a real machine.

Our Mission

Make pharmaceutical instrumentation education accessible to every student, everywhere.

Our Vision

A world where no pharmacy graduate faces an instrument they haven't already mastered virtually.
