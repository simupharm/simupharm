### Standard Scoring Algorithm (Brooke 1996):
- For odd items ($1, 3, 5, 7, 9$): Score contribution $= 	ext{Scale Position} - 1$
- For even items ($2, 4, 6, 8, 10$): Score contribution $= 5 - 	ext{Scale Position}$
- Individual SUS Score $= 2.5 	imes \sum_{i=1}^{10} 	ext{Contribution}_i \in [0, 100]$
- Continuous interpretation against Bangor et al. (2008) empirical thresholds:
  - $< 50$: Not Acceptable
  - $50 - 69$: Marginal
  - $70 - 79$: Acceptable / Good
  - $\ge 80$: Excellent
  - $\ge 85$: Best Imaginable
